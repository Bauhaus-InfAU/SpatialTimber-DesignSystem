Text highlight · brick on cream

We can densify without distribution layers — the wall-beam carries load directly.
The brick red is the only color used for emphasis in running text. It marks load-bearing terms (the wall-beam idea, the design-coupling gap, the surrogate) and KPI figures. Every other color stays inside graphics.
Body text on oat · ink #141413 · WCAG AAA at all body sizes

For multi-category charts, draw in the order peach → clay → olive → sky. Clay-interactive is reserved for highlights and the wall-beam, and should not appear as a chart category.

Pairing rules

1 · Clay does double duty

Use #D97757 on text highlights, KPI numbers, force-arrow indicators, the wall-beam structural elements in axos, and as the warm-mid chart category. The single warm-orange anchors the visual identity across both text and graphics.

2 · Ink is gray-950, not pure black

Body text, axo outlines, and axis ticks use #141413. It's near-black but warm-toned, keeping coherence with the oat paper.

3 · Stay inside the Anthropic palette

Olive and sky are deliberately desaturated to share temperature with the warm tones. Don't substitute with vivid greens or cyans — they will read as imports.
