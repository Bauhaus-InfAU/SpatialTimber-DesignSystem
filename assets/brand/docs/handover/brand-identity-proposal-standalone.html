A logo system that stacks.

One master mark, an extensible family of sub-project lockups, and the rule for building the next one. Precise, structural, timber-warm — built to live beside the project deck and app.

01 — The mark

Stacked timber storeys, in axonometric.

The signature idea — adding a new timber storey on top of an existing building — drawn literally as stacked structural slabs. The topmost storey, the one being added, carries the clay accent.

One idea, executed precisely
Aufstockung, abstracted to its structure

Three slabs read as storeys; the axonometric view makes it spatial; the snug stack with one new layer on top is Aufstockung. It reduces to a single silhouette at 16 px and works in one ink colour — clay is optional.

The footprint sits on the project's 625 mm structural module: equal width slabs, equal storey rhythm, no decoration.

03 — Wordmark

Set in Inter, weight on Spatial.

All one word — the capital T is the only case signal, so it reads as a single product name. The weight now sits on Spatial — the project's core idea — with Timber stepping back to medium. The weight-shift treatment is canonical; it carries the emphasis without colour.

04 — Sub-project family

One scheme, swappable module.
